# Hospital — Patient Risk Analysis

### Real-world problem

A hospital wants to identify patients who may have higher health risk based on age, blood pressure and sugar level.

**We need to find out:**

- Average age
- Average blood pressure
- Average sugar level
- High-risk patients
- Relationship between age and blood pressure
- Relationship between sugar and blood pressure
